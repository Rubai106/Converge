# CONVERGE — Competitor Validation

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Method:** web search + vendor-page opens. Prior-research claims re-checked where a homepage was reachable; gaps are stated, not assumed. No "CONVERGE is first" claim is made — the honest claim is narrower, per workflow.

## Verdict

Almost every named competitor exists — except **Sofra (as procurement)** and **Happi Happi**, which could not be found and should be removed from the pitch. **HAGGLE is the most crowded**: invoice→dish-margin is a solved $59/mo commodity (PlateSense/Marji) and autonomous negotiation is shipped at enterprise scale (Pactum) and for restaurants (SupplyScout). **COURT is the least crowded**: no one adjudicates conflicting versions of one dish. **CORRECTION is split**: transcript editing/show notes are solved (Riverside/Descript), but no product lets the *guest* correct timestamped claims in chat before publish. CONVERGE's defensible claim is the **assembly**: one shared decision engine, in-chat multi-party work, deterministic maths/evidence, and a human approval gate, in Mattermost via Switch, for users who will never install a portal.

## What not to claim

1. Not "first" at recipe costing, supplier negotiation, blind tasting, or podcast fact-checking — each component is shipped somewhere.
2. Not statistically valid sensory science for COURT — a family panel is a deliberation tool, not a study; say so.
3. Not a real supplier agreement unless a real supplier accepted it — label the demo supplier as simulated.
4. Do not cite **Sofra** or **Happi Happi** — not found, likely invented/misnamed. Do not cite **Cupi's** chat-approval flow until its homepage is opened — company exists, that feature is unverified in this pass.
5. The shared-engine claim is the defensible one: one decision engine (case → evidence → conflict → proposal → approval → audited outcome) reused across three workflows, with Switch rooms as the collaboration surface and deterministic tools for money/maths. No named competitor offers that *combination* in Mattermost via Switch for these users — phrase it as an assembly claim, not a first-ever claim.
